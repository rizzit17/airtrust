## Gaps that determine the experiments

Hu, Putra and Alwabli motivate federated pollution prediction, but their reported forecasting settings do not establish the performance of our model at completely unseen Delhi locations. Five station folds test that question with the same future-time boundary.

Ye, Mengistu and Khan distinguish statistical diversity from sensing reliability. We therefore compare sample count, physical co-location residuals, robust updates and update consistency rather than treating non-IID distributions as sensor faults.

Zheng shows that calibration depends on location, reference instrument and environmental conditions. Our virtual co-location experiment isolates weighting from spatial gradients, while an input-calibration ablation tests whether correcting measurements helps more than weighting alone. Delhi hardware validation remains a separate requirement for deployment.

Liu and Wu explain why communication structure and cryptographic privacy matter. We measure logical payload and a specified membership probe, and avoid claiming a privacy guarantee or radio-energy saving without the corresponding protocol.
